## Introduction

Domainstack ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our domain intelligence platform.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an account, we collect the following details from the login provider you choose:

- Name
- Email address (with verification status)
- Profile picture (optional)
- Identifiers from your login provider (account ID) and the access tokens it issues, stored encrypted

### Domain Data

When you use our service, we collect and display the following publicly available information:

- Domain registration and WHOIS information
- DNS records
- SSL/TLS certificate information
- HTTP headers and hosting information
- SEO metadata and screenshots of websites

### Usage Data

We automatically collect:

- Pages viewed and features used
- Device and browser information
- IP address and approximate location

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain our service
- Send domain and certificate expiration notifications
- Improve and personalize your experience
- Communicate important updates about our service
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Domain data is cached temporarily to improve performance and is refreshed periodically.

You can delete your account at any time through the self-service option in your dashboard settings. Account deletion is permanent. It removes your account, tracked domains, notification preferences, and subscription records from our database, deletes your customer record at Polar, and removes you from our Resend contact list. Analytics records held by PostHog and authentication event logs held by Better Auth Infrastructure are kept according to those providers' retention settings. To have them deleted, email [privacy@domainstack.io](mailto:privacy@domainstack.io).

## Payment Information

A customer record with your name and email address is created at Polar when you sign up, so a subscription can be started later. When you subscribe to our Pro plan, payment processing is handled by our third-party partners:

- **[Polar](https://polar.sh)** — Our merchant of record for subscription management. Polar collects and processes your payment information, including billing address and payment method. See [Polar's Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy).
- **[Stripe](https://stripe.com)** — Payment processing infrastructure. Stripe handles your payment card data securely. See [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

We do not store your full payment card details. We receive only limited information from our payment partners, such as subscription status, billing email, and the last four digits of your card for display purposes.

## Email Communications

We use [Resend](https://resend.com) to send transactional emails, including:

- Domain and certificate expiration alerts
- Domain verification status notifications
- Subscription and account-related emails
- Account deletion confirmation emails

When we send you an email, your email address is shared with Resend for delivery purposes. When you sign up, your name and email address are also added to our contact list at Resend (used for product updates you can opt out of), moved to your new address if you change it (keeping your opt-out choice), and removed when you delete your account. When you change your email address, we send a confirmation link to the new address and a notice to the old one. See [Resend's Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## AI Assistant

We offer an optional AI-powered chat assistant ("Stacky") to help you look up domain information using natural language.

### How It Works

The AI assistant is powered by [Vercel AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway), which routes Domainstack's requests to large language model (LLM) providers. When you send a message:

- Your message and recent conversation history (up to 10 messages) are sent to the AI service
- The request to Vercel AI Gateway includes your account ID if you're signed in, or your IP address if you're not
- The domain you're currently viewing may be included as context
- The AI may use our domain lookup tools to fetch publicly available information

An optional "Local" mode runs the AI model on your device instead. In Local mode, your messages are not sent to AI providers.

### Conversation Storage

Your conversations are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This means:

- Clearing your browser data will delete your chat history
- Conversations do not sync across devices or browsers
- You can clear your chat history at any time using the clear button

Conversations are also handled on our side when you use the cloud assistant:

- Each message you send is processed in a Vercel Workflow run. The record of that run includes the conversation, your IP address, and your account ID if you're signed in.
- We record each assistant response in PostHog, including the conversation sent to the model and the model's reply, to monitor quality and cost. These records are linked to your account ID if you're signed in.

### Data Shared with AI Providers

When you use the AI assistant, your messages are processed by third-party AI providers through Vercel AI Gateway. These providers have their own privacy policies:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) (for OpenAI models)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) (for Gemini models)
- [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy) (for Claude models)
- [Vercel Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)

The provider(s) used to process your conversations will vary.

## Analytics

We use [PostHog](https://posthog.com) for product analytics and error tracking to understand how our service is used and to improve the user experience. PostHog collects:

- Pages viewed and features used
- Device and browser information
- IP address (used for approximate location, then discarded)
- Your name and email address, if you're signed in
- AI assistant conversations and responses (see AI Assistant above)
- Error reports and performance metrics

Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve our service and is not sold to third parties. See [PostHog's Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Vercel:** Hosting, image storage (Vercel Blob), AI Gateway, and Workflow
- **PostHog:** Product analytics, error tracking, and AI assistant analytics (see Analytics section above)
- **Better Auth Infrastructure:** Authentication event monitoring and security. It receives sign-up, sign-in, session, and account deletion events with your user ID, name, email address, IP address, and approximate location.
- **Upstash:** Session storage and rate limiting, keyed by your account ID or IP address
- **Resend:** Email delivery and our contact list (see Email Communications section above)
- **Polar and Stripe:** Billing (see Payment Information section above)
- **AI model providers via Vercel AI Gateway:** Processing AI assistant messages (see AI Assistant section above)
- **CARTO and unpkg:** Map tiles and the map library, loaded directly by your browser on domain report pages, so they can see your IP address
- **Legal requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data — You can permanently delete your account at any time from your dashboard settings. This removes your account, tracked domains, notification preferences, and subscription records from our database, deletes your customer record at Polar, and removes you from our Resend contact list. If you have an active subscription, it will be automatically canceled. To have your analytics records at PostHog and authentication event logs at Better Auth Infrastructure deleted, email [privacy@domainstack.io](mailto:privacy@domainstack.io).
- Export your data
- Opt out of marketing communications

## Cookies

We use cookies to provide and improve our service. Our approach to cookies depends on your location:

### For EU/EEA Visitors

If you visit from a country covered by GDPR (EU member states, EEA countries, and the UK), you'll see a cookie consent banner on your first visit. You can choose to:

- **Accept:** We'll use analytics cookies to understand how you use our service and improve your experience.
- **Decline:** We'll switch to cookieless tracking mode, collecting only anonymized usage data without storing any cookies on your device.

### For Visitors Outside the EU/EEA

If you visit from outside the EU/EEA, analytics cookies are enabled by default to provide the best experience. You can still opt out using your browser's built-in cookie controls.

### Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential cookies:** Required for basic functionality like authentication and session management. These are always active regardless of your consent choice.
- **Analytics cookies:** Used by PostHog to understand how you use our service, track errors, and improve the user experience. For EU/EEA visitors, these require your consent.

### Changing Your Preferences

To change your cookie preferences, you can clear your browser's cookies and local storage for this site, then revisit to see the consent banner again (EU/EEA visitors only).
